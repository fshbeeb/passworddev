Portfolio

Password Dev publishes Foo Guard, Expensy AI, Alineana, and fshbeeb. Each one has its own website.

01

Foo Guard

AI agent security

Foo Guard reviews the files that describe an AI agent before that agent is deployed. A scan looks at identity, tools, permissions, credentials, and autonomy. Scores come from a deterministic rules engine.

  • Configuration scanning
  • Library of reviewed configs and skills
  • Hosted gateway for routed requests
  • GitHub and CI policy checks
fooguard.com (opens in a new tab)

02

Expensy AI

Expense tracking

Photograph a receipt and Expensy reads the merchant, date, total, and a category. Mileage and meeting minutes stay with the person who submitted them. In a team, the owner can see the whole workspace. Everyone else sees their own.

  • Receipt capture and categories
  • Mileage
  • Meeting minutes
  • Team workspaces
expensyai.com (opens in a new tab)

03

Alineana

AI chart analysis

Photograph a stock chart, or choose a screenshot. Alineana detects the pattern and the trend, writes the analysis, and gives a recommendation. The news moving the market is in the same check. Alineana does not place trades and does not connect to a brokerage.

  • Pattern and trend from the photo
  • Written analysis and a recommendation
  • Market news in the same check
  • Coming soon on iPhone
alineana.com (opens in a new tab)

04

fshbeeb

Invite-only society

fshbeeb is an ultra-exclusive, invite-only society. Membership is by invitation.

  • Invite only
  • Entry through fshbeeb.com

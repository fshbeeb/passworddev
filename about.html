What the company does

Password Dev designs, publishes, and operates its own software. The company runs the products, the accounts, and the support for those products.

Foo Guard, at fooguard.com, is an AI agent security scanner. It reviews the files that describe an agent before that agent is deployed: identity, tools, permissions, credentials, and autonomy. Scores come from a deterministic rules engine. Teams can keep reviewed configurations, monitor routed requests, and fail a check when policy does not pass.

Expensy AI, at expensyai.com, is expense tracking. A receipt photo becomes a merchant, date, total, and category. Mileage and meeting minutes stay with the person who submitted them. In a team, the owner can see the whole workspace, and everyone else sees their own.

Alineana, at alineana.com, reads a photo of a stock chart. It detects the pattern and the trend, writes the analysis, and gives a recommendation, with the news moving the market in the same check. The iPhone app is coming soon. Alineana does not place trades and does not connect to a brokerage.

fshbeeb, at fshbeeb.com, is an ultra-exclusive, invite-only society. Membership is by invitation.

Each one has its own website. Password Dev, LLC is the company that publishes them.
